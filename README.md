Professional portfolio for the Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence at Indiana Wesleyan University (AIML-500: AI Lab). It showcases my professional bio, my personal value proposition and course projects that demonstrate how I apply AI concepts in real-world settings.

## About me

I am a Senior Data Scientist at CVS Health in Caremark's Pricing and Product Analytics team, where I build pricing and optimization models for pharmacy benefits. I hold a PhD in Systems Engineering from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Before CVS Health, I was a Senior Data Scientist at ComEd, and earlier an aerospace engineer at the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO).

**Value proposition:** I help healthcare and analytics teams turn complex, high-stakes data into AI and pricing decisions they can trust.

## Repository structure

```
index.html                         Home: bio, value proposition, project list
projects/
  reddit-stock-chatbot.html        Project 1: AI Lab chatbot
  seasons-of-ai.html               Project 2: AI and ML timeline
assets/
  css/site.css                     Site styles (light and dark themes)
  theme.js                         Theme toggle
  img/                             Screenshots and figures
  files/                           Downloadable deliverables (report, bot instructions, slides)
```

## Adding a new project

1. Copy a page in `projects/` and replace its content, keeping the template sections.
2. Add images to `assets/img/` and downloadable files to `assets/files/`.
3. Add a `project-row` entry to the project list in `index.html`.
4. Commit and push; GitHub Pages republishes in about a minute.
